06 / Questions

Good intelligence starts with good questions.

Is this another tender-alert service?

No. The proposed service focuses on early signals and commercial preparation, not only published tenders.

Are all signals open opportunities?

No. Some are early indicators, planning developments or reasons to investigate.

Which regions and government bodies are covered?

Aheadmark Scout is initially intended for NSW cleaning and facilities services. Specific coverage is agreed on onboarding.

How do you decide whether something is relevant?

We assess evidence strength, procurement stage and supplier fit separately.

Can I inspect the supporting evidence?

Yes, the intended brief includes source references and a clear distinction between fact and interpretation. Preview examples still require source verification.

What happens when there are no relevant signals?

A brief can report that no relevant signals were identified within the agreed scope rather than invent opportunities.

Does this guarantee contracts or advance access?

No. Aheadmark helps prioritise research and commercial preparation using public information. It does not provide privileged access, guarantee future procurement or replace formal purchasing processes.
